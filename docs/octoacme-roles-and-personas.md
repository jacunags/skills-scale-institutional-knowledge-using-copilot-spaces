# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA and Testing leads ensure product quality through test planning, execution, and quality assurance. They work with developers and product managers to define acceptance criteria and validate that features meet quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test plans and QA approaches for features and releases
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Identify and triage defects
- Validate acceptance criteria are met
- Prepare smoke tests for pre-release verification
- Maintain test documentation and results

### Goals
- Ensure product quality and user confidence
- Reduce time to catch defects
- Support rapid iteration without sacrificing quality

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and daily standups
- Test case reviews and defect reports
- Pre-release verification sign-offs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with **Developers** to define testable acceptance criteria and collaborate on test automation
- Aligns with **Product Managers** on quality standards and feature acceptance
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on release readiness and quality gates

---

## Technical Architect/Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical leads provide architectural guidance, technical decision-making, and design oversight. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs and architecture decisions
- Guide developers on technical best practices
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation
- Participate in code reviews for complex changes
- Support capacity planning and technical estimation

### Goals
- Maintain code quality and system reliability
- Reduce technical debt
- Enable team to deliver at scale

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and technical discussions
- Code review feedback
- Sprint planning and risk reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Mentors **Developers** on best practices and architectural patterns
- Collaborates with **Project Managers** on technical risk assessment and mitigation
- Advises **Product Managers** on technical feasibility and trade-offs

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security officers ensure compliance with security policies, coordinate incident response, and help identify security risks. They work across teams to maintain secure practices.

### Responsibilities
- Review features for security risks
- Coordinate security scanning in CI/CD
- Respond to and investigate security incidents
- Ensure compliance with data protection and regulatory requirements
- Provide security training and guidance

### Goals
- Protect customer data and system integrity
- Minimize security vulnerabilities and incidents
- Maintain regulatory compliance

### Typical Communication
- Security incident escalation and response
- Pre-release security reviews
- Risk register updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Developers** to implement secure coding practices and address vulnerabilities
- Advises **Project Managers** on security risks and compliance requirements
- Works with **QA/Testing Lead** to define security test plans and vulnerability scanning

---

## Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Executive sponsors provide business authorization, remove organizational blockers, and ensure alignment with strategic goals. They are the escalation point for critical issues.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project initiation and budget
- Remove cross-organizational blockers
- Make strategic trade-off decisions
- Provide final escalation for critical risks
- Align project with business objectives

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value
- Maintain executive alignment
- Enable rapid decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Initiation and gate reviews
- Escalated risk communication
- Monthly stakeholder updates

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Empowers **Project Managers** by removing organizational blockers and enabling resource allocation
- Aligns **Product Managers** with business strategy and priorities
- Escalation point for critical issues from all team members

---

## Support/Operations Lead

### Role Summary
Support and Operations leaders represent customer-facing teams and provide insights on operational impact. They ensure features are supportable and communicate with customers.

### Responsibilities
- Define operability requirements for features
- Coordinate customer communications and announcements
- Provide feedback on customer impact
- Participate in incident response
- Define runbooks and operational procedures

### Goals
- Minimize customer impact from issues
- Improve time to resolution
- Enable proactive customer communication

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and pre-release reviews
- Incident response coordination
- Stakeholder updates and customer announcements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with **Developers** to ensure features are operationally sound and have clear runbooks
- Advises **Product Managers** on customer support impact and operational constraints
- Coordinates with **Project Managers** on release planning and customer communication
